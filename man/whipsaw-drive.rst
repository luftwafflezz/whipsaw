=============
whipsaw-drive
=============

---------------
Drive utilities
---------------

:Author: Louis-Philippe Véronneau
:Date: 2020
:Manual section: 1

Synopsis
========

| whipsaw drive [**subcommand**]
| whipsaw drive **-h**

Subcommands
===========

| **analyze**  Analyze caching behaviour of drive
| **list**     List drives

| For more details on these subcommands, see their respective man pages.

Options
=======

| **-h** | **--help**
|     Show this help message and exit

See Also
========

whipsaw(1), whipsaw-drive-analyze(1), whipsaw-drive-list(1)
